# Song breakdown (supplied with the revised treatment)

## 1. Core musical identity
- **Genre:** anthemic gothic rock / dark alternative.
- **Tempo & feel:** driving and relentless but heavily punctuated; a heavy four-on-the-floor rock feel in the choruses, with extreme dynamic range.
- **Vocal delivery:** a deep, booming, theatrical baritone, shifting from an intimate, dangerous whisper to a massive, commanding roar.

## 2. Thematic duality: vulnerability vs. predation
- **The words** are a desperate plea for unconditional love ("Lieb mich wie ich bin").
- **The delivery** is a hunter's, not a victim's: the verses compare the narrator to a panther tracking its prey, a stalker following every step.
- **Creative translation:** a lethal targeting sequence; the demand for love is an inescapable system override.

## 3. Structure and dynamics
- **The void (verses):** suffocating tension, apnea, the calm before a strike ("Ich halt den Atem an"). Extreme minimalism: an EKG flatline on black; LIDAR scans searching for a thermal signature.
- **The whisper (pre-chorus):** "Sanft singt der Rabe im Wind" — dark romanticism, elegant but chaotic; a maelstrom of data (boids, strange attractors) swallowing the camera before the drop.
- **The command (chorus):** a wall of guitars, cymbals and kicks; LIEB MICH slams in filling 90% of the frame; blinding crimson flashes on the heavy downbeats; target locks clamping on the snares; screen shake tearing the codec apart.

## 4. Visual vocabulary
Void, ash, bone, crimson (the only colour that blooms). Lethal precision: hard snaps, tight springs, whip-pans; if the camera moves, it is dragged by an inescapable force. Words are physical objects that stretch, compress and break the screen limits.

## 5. Emotional target
The viewer should feel hunted, overwhelmed and breathless. The transitions from pitch-black silence to blinding typography should feel like a physical assault.
